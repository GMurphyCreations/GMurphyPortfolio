About

I'm Gavin, based in Santa Barbara. I've spent the last seven years in creative marketing on both sides of the table. In-house as Creative Marketing Lead at Raw Garden, and independently shooting content for clients like Mind Garden, Cold Call, and Cortex. Plus Savor, a retail delivery brand I co-founded and grew from zero through social alone. Building brands in the cannabis space taught me to grow audiences the hard way. Strict ad restrictions mean organic content, community, and creative that earns attention on its own.

Off the clock I digitize Hi8 and MiniDV tape on vintage camcorders and FireWire era Macs. An analog habit that keeps my eye honest. And I chase snow whenever the season allows.

Now: looking to bring that full-stack range in-house to a marketing, social media, or content production team in the Santa Barbara area. Also booking freelance photo and content work.
